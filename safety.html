🛡️ Travel Safety Tips

  • Always keep a photocopy of your passport and store the digital copy in cloud storage.
  • Share your live location and itinerary with a trusted person back home.
  • Keep emergency cash (₹2,000–5,000) separate from your main wallet.
  • Avoid travelling alone at night in unfamiliar areas — use registered cabs or hotel transport.
  • Purchase travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and baggage loss.
  • Save the local emergency numbers in your phone before you land — do not rely on memory.
